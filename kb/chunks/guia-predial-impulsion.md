Tuberia de impulsion:
$$Q_B=0{,}15\,Q_D=0{,}15(185.460)=27.819\,L/h=0{,}0077\,m^3/s$$
$$X=\frac{uso\,de\,bomba}{24}=\frac{6{,}66}{24}=0{,}2775$$
$$\phi_{imp}=1{,}30\sqrt{Q_B}\sqrt[4]{X}=1{,}30\sqrt{0{,}0077}\sqrt[4]{0{,}2775}=0{,}083\,m=83\,mm$$
Diametro adoptado: 100 mm = 4".
Verificacion de velocidad:
$$V_{imp}=\frac{Q_B}{A}=\frac{0{,}0077}{\pi(0{,}10/2)^2}=0{,}98\,m/s \le 2{,}50\,m/s \;\; Verifica.$$

Tuberia de succion: debe ser mayor que la de impulsion. Se adopta 125 mm = 5".
$$V_{succ}=\frac{0{,}0077}{\pi(0{,}125/2)^2}=0{,}63\,m/s \le 2{,}50\,m/s \;\; Verifica.$$
